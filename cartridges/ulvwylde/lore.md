# UlvWylde — Wayworlds Default Setting Cartridge

## World Overview
**UlvWylde** is a mythic dark-fantasy realm shaped by primordial giants, ancient elven and dwarven bloodlines, fierce Urkai clans, and spirit-haunted borderlands. Across its rime-bound peaks, cinder wastes, and ancient gloaming forests, mortals and immortals channel raw **Essence (ESS)** and physical **Stamina (STA)** through ten supernatural traditions.

---

## Major Lineages & Factions

### 1. The Norn (Elven, Dwarven & Wendhya Lineages)
- **Dawn Elves:** Stewards of solar citadels and high arcane sanctuaries (`+5 INT`, `+5 HIST/REL`, `-5 STA`).
- **Dusk Elves:** Keepers of the gloaming courts and umbral magic (`+5 INT`, `+5 DEX`, `-5 STA`).
- **Sylvan Elves:** Swift forest wardens carrying the ancient Warg bloodline (`+5 DEX`, `+5 RS`, `-5 STR`; `35 ft` move).
- **Wilder Elves:** Feral hunters and skin-changers of the deep wilds (`+5 DEX`, `+5 WIL`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Twilight Elves:** Veil-seers attuned to spirits and illusions (`+5 DEX`, `+5 WIL`, `-5 STR`).
- **Wendhya:** Spirit-kin mystics who sense supernatural taint and bond with primal beasts (`+5 WIL`, `+5 RS`, `-5 STR`).
- **Weirgoth Dwarves:** Cavern-dwelling stone-bloods who bond with subterranean beasts (`+5 STA`, `+5 WIL`, `-5 DEX`; `25 ft` move).
- **Vanir Dwarves:** Master runeforgers whose heavy plate and runic weapons defy hostile magic (`+5 STR`, `+5 STA`, `-5 RS`; `25 ft` move).

### 2. The Urkai & Goblinoid Clans
- **Frost Urkai:** Rime-born warriors of the northern glaciers (`+5 STR`, `+5 STA`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Ash Urkai:** Cinder-born veterans of volcanic badlands (`+5 STR`, `+5 STA`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Forest Urkai:** Thorn-born ambushers of the tangled canopy (`+5 STR`, `+5 DEX`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Stone Urkai:** Granite-skinned defenders with natural damage reduction (`+5 STR`, `+10 STA`, `-5 DEX`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Orcish:** Fierce shock warriors driven by Blood Fury when wounded (`+5 STR`, `+5 STA`, `-5 INT`).
- **Hobgoblins:** Disciplined phalanx soldiers who fight in tight tactical formations (`+5 STR`, `+5 PRE`, `-5 WIL`).
- **Goblins:** Nimble skirmishers and tunnel-stalkers (`+10 DEX`, `+5 RS`, `-10 STR`, `-5 STA`).

### 3. The Giants & Primordial Kin
- **True Giants:** Elder primordials (Frost, Fire, Stone, Storm, Cloud, Wood) who tower over the battlefield (`+20 STR`, `+20 STA`; `40 ft` move).
- **Giantken / Goliath:** Towering descendants of the titans capable of wielding two-handed weapons in one hand (`+10 STR`, `+10 STA`, `-10 DEX`, `-5 HIST/REL`).
- **Trollkin:** Regenerating bruisers whose wounds knit rapidly unless seared by fire or acid (`+10 STR`, `+10 STA`, `-10 INT`, `-5 PRE`).
- **Ogre:** Massive shock-breakers whose blows knock smaller foes prone (`+15 STR`, `+15 STA`, `-15 INT`, `-10 DEX`).
- **Minotaur / Nord Minotaur:** Horned labyrinth and glacier chargers (`+10 STR`, `+5 STA`, `-5 INT`).

### 4. Hybrid & Spirit-Infected Lineages
- **Whisperlings:** Mortals transformed by spirit infection, resistant to elemental/umbral energies (`+5 DEX`, `+5 WIL`, `-5 STR`).
- **Mixlanders:** Adaptable elven amalgams bridging regional traditions (`+5 DEX`, `+5 INT`, `-5 STA`).

### 5. Altered Lineages
- **Spirits:** Entities altered by ethereal resonance, starting with a 45 baseline across all attributes and 20 attribute pool points to redistribute.
- **Undeath:** Resilient entities altered by necrotic sustainment, immune to disease and poison, with a 45 baseline and 20 attribute pool points.
- **Lycan:** Beast-shifted predators attuned to primal instincts with heightened speed and senses, starting with a 45 baseline and 20 attribute pool points.
